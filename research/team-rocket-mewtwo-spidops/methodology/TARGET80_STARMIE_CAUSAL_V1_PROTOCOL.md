against the two exact Starmie identities implicated by the live V6 losses. It
replaces sparse terminal-reward PPO with paired action values at identical
official-engine roots. It cannot authorize packaging, arena submission, an
80% claim, hidden-hand belief claims, or a game-theoretic safety claim.

official engine. The Starmie entrypoint executes in the arena evaluator's
isolated packaged-policy worker. Before every branch, the worker is reset and
all earlier source-match observations are replayed; every replayed action must
match the source action exactly. The transient visualizer truth is used only
to initialize the official branch at the one realized hidden completion. Raw
hidden zones are not serialized and are never passed to the Rocket policy.

This remains realized-state causal evidence, not an information-set expected
value. The final strategic architecture remains the public-belief blueprint
plus safe re-solving boundary documented elsewhere in this project; sampled
rollouts are supervision, not the final policy.

## Discovery rule

Analysis may group roots only by public, deployable semantic conditions. A
candidate correction is eligible for a new, separately named development bot
only if all of the following hold:

- at least six supporting roots and at least three complete source games;
- support in both Starmie rows, or an exact public card-family discriminator;
- paired mean terminal gain of at least `+0.50` on changed roots;
- positive paired outcome in at least two thirds of changed roots;
- negative paired outcome in at most one sixth of changed roots;
- the action is legal and expressible solely from the normal observation; and
- it is materially different from every closed Starmie attachment, broad
  Bangle, PPO, finish-action, and hand-authored intervention already recorded.

The smallest qualifying public rule wins. Ties break by larger all-root gain,
then more games, then lexical semantic signature. If no rule qualifies, close
the panel and do not mine weaker thresholds.
