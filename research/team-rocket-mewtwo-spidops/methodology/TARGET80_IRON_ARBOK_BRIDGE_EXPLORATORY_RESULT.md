# Target-80 Iron/Arbok bridge exploratory result

Status: **abandoned before preregistration; never eligible for submission**  
Reviewed: 2026-08-04

The legal-card audit confirmed that Active Iron Thorns ex suppresses the Rule
Box Ability on Marnie's Grimmsnarl ex while leaving Team Rocket's Spidops's
non-Rule Box Charging Up intact. An exploratory Wobbuffet-free package combined
Iron Thorns, Team Rocket's Arbok, Brock's Scouting, Ultra Ball, Switch, and
Scramble Switch.

The mechanism fired in official-engine smokes, including games where
Grimmsnarl evolved while Iron Thorns was Active and therefore missed Punk Up.
The package nevertheless produced the wrong exchange pattern:

- Iron Thorns and Arbok both carry Abilities, so Froslass continued placing
  damage counters on them;
- Munkidori could continue moving damage counters because it is not a Rule Box
  Pokemon;
- Iron Thorns consumed a non-Rocket board slot and often several turns before
  an exit card appeared; and
- the extra lock/search package reduced Spidops evolution and prize pressure.

After one early legality win, four successive decision-review smokes of the
iterated package lost to the exact Marnie opponent. Those games are neither a
strength estimate nor a locked panel, but their complete traces showed a
structural mechanism failure rather than a single parameter miss. The package
was therefore abandoned before a frozen development protocol. It must not be
renamed, packaged, submitted, or represented as an evaluated candidate.

Exploratory artifacts remain under
`work/target80_candidates/iron_arbok_bridge_v1/` and
`work/target80_iron_arbok_smoke_v1*/` for negative-transfer evidence.

No Kaggle or internal-arena submission was made.
