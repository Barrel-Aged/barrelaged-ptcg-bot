# Target-80 Hancang structural deck V1 result

Status: **closed under the frozen transferred policy; no submission**  
Recorded: 2026-08-05

## Result

The exact public Hancang recovery list was directionally stronger than the flg
V6 deck under the same frozen model and guards, but it was nowhere near the
preregistered absolute-strength gate:

| Arm | W-L-D | Win rate | Starts first | Starts second |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Hancang structural candidate | 63-48-1 | 56.25% | 34/56 | 29/56 |
| flg control | 58-53-1 | 51.79% | 31/56 | 27/56 |

The candidate gained five wins (`+4.46` points), one win short of the locked
six-win comparison gate. More importantly, it missed the `84/112` absolute
gate by 21 wins and missed both `40/56` start-order gates.

## Integrity and claim boundary

All 14 manifests and 224 game rows passed:

- exact collector, deck, model, and entrypoint identities;
- 8 candidate plus 8 control games per row;
- seven opponents and both fixed start orders;
- genuine bundled official-engine terminal execution;
- isolated packaged policies and strict legality;
- zero illegal actions, exception fallbacks, timeouts, or incomplete games;
- zero Wobbuffet; and
- matching JSONL hashes.

The two selected public Kaggle wins remain provenance only. They were not part
of this panel and do not alter the result.

## Decision

Close the exact Hancang deck with the frozen transferred ScaleBC policy. Do not
open Stage B, package it, submit it, or weaken the gates. The +4.46-point signal
is useful evidence that Night Stretcher/energy/Bangle recovery is more coherent
than the flg density package, but policy transfer leaves the candidate at
ordinary arena strength rather than plausible 80% strength.

A genuinely new policy trained for this exact list would be a different
hypothesis and would require new preregistration. V5 and V6 remain immutable;
no Kaggle submission was made.
