## Admission gates

All gates are required for a deck to advance to exact-deck learning:

1. Exact 60-card deck, zero Wobbuffet, correct source model, deterministic
   serialization, and zero engine, import, legality, timeout, or fallback
   errors.
2. The intended attacker reaches play in at least 9/12 routed games and becomes
   attack-ready in at least 7/12.
3. The intended attack is used in at least 6/12 games, including at least two
   games from each seat and two against each Starmie policy.
4. At least four intended attacks either take a Prize or deal at least 200
   damage. For Zapdos, at least two attacks must have both Team Rocket's Energy
   and Maximum Belt attached. For Galvantula, `Charged Web` itself must occur in
   at least four games; `Jolting Charge` alone is insufficient.
5. The routed arm must win at least 5/12 and must not trail its deck-identical
   control by more than one win. This is a gross-collapse filter, not evidence
   of deployment strength.

If both pass, select lexicographically by intended-attack games, qualifying
damage/Prize events, wins, ready-attacker games, and lower median first-attack
turn. Freeze at most one deck for learning. If neither passes, close both exact
packages and return to a fresh deck-level screen; do not tune thresholds after
opening results.

## Required later stages

The selected deck must be trained and calibrated on that exact 60-card
identity. The final runtime may use an autoregressive learned policy, public-
belief/value layer, or safe information-set re-solving interface, but not the
mechanism wrapper as its strategic endpoint. It must pass a fresh side-swapped
broad panel including both Starmies, Marnie, Rocket, Alakazam, Crustle, and
resource-wall/generalist opponents. Only one immutable successor may enter an
untouched 200-game local confirmation, which must reach at least 160 wins.
Only then may it be submitted to the internal arena, where that exact identity
must independently reach at least 160 wins in its first 200 rated games.
